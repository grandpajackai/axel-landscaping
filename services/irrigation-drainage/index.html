Irrigation & drainage.

AXEL DESIGN INSPIRATION

THOUGHTFUL BY DESIGN

Good landscapes begin below the surface.

Too little water stresses a garden; too much can undermine it. We review how water arrives, where it collects and how it moves across the site. The goal is a practical solution that works with the property and the landscape around it.

Compacted or clay-heavy soil can make drainage a key consideration in Kansas City. Every property is different, so recommendations begin with site conditions and a conversation about the problems you are seeing.

WHAT WE CAN BRING TO YOUR SPACE

  • Irrigation system planning & installation
  • Sprinkler troubleshooting & repairs
  • Controller & watering schedule updates
  • Drip irrigation for planting beds
  • Surface drainage & runoff management
  • Downspout drainage & site grading coordination

Your proposal defines the services, materials and scope specific to your property.

Can you help with a soggy area in my yard?

Describe where water collects and when it happens. A site assessment helps determine whether grading, runoff routing or a drainage installation is appropriate.

Can irrigation be updated without replacing everything?

In some cases, repairs, controller adjustments or a targeted zone update can improve an existing system. The right scope depends on its condition.
