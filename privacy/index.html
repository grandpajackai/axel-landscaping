YOUR INFORMATION, HANDLED WITH CARE

Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to information you enter on the Axel Landscaping website and choose to share with us.

LAST UPDATED OCTOBER 12, 2026

Your project brief stays in your browser

The project form helps you prepare a brief with your name, email address, optional phone number, property ZIP code, services of interest, budget, timeline and project ideas. Preparing the brief does not submit an inquiry or send these details to Axel Landscaping or a website database.

Your entries are held temporarily in this page’s browser memory. The website does not save them in cookies, local storage or a server database. Leaving or refreshing the page clears this temporary form state. Your browser may separately offer its own autofill or restore features, which are controlled by your browser settings.

You choose when to share

The copy button places your brief on your device’s clipboard when you activate it. If automatic copying is unavailable, you can select and copy the text yourself. Your device controls how clipboard contents are retained or shared.

The text link opens a draft in a compatible messaging app, with Axel Landscaping’s number and your brief filled in. Opening that draft does not send it. Review the message and use your app’s send button if you wish to contact Axel. Your messaging app and mobile provider handle any message you send under their own terms and privacy practices. Calls are also handled through your device and telephone provider.

Information you send directly to Axel

If you choose to send a text or call, Axel receives the information you share through that conversation. We use it to discuss your project, confirm service availability and respond to your request. Sharing a project brief does not enroll you in a marketing campaign.

Please include only details relevant to your landscape project. Do not include payment information or sensitive personal information in your brief.

Website hosting and technical information

This presentation website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information needed to deliver, secure and operate its hosting service, which may include your IP address and information supplied by your browser. Learn more in GitHub’s privacy statement.

This website does not include advertising trackers, marketing cookies or visitor analytics tools. Its project form does not transmit your entries to the hosting service.

Fonts and external websites

Fonts are delivered by Google Fonts. Loading them connects your browser to Google, which receives technical information such as your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for its practices.

Field notes may link to external educational resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them. This website does not collect payment details or process purchases.

Your choices and questions

You can leave optional fields blank, edit your brief before sharing it, or contact Axel directly without using the form. To ask about information you have shared by phone or text, request a correction or deletion, or tell us you no longer wish to discuss a project, call (913) 954-3911. We may need to confirm your identity and retain information where required for relevant business or legal records.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or our information practices change. The date above reflects the latest revision.
