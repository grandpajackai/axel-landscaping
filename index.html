BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few good
questions.

Something else on your mind?

Where does Axel Landscaping work?

We serve Kansas City and the surrounding metro. Share your property ZIP code when you get in touch so we can confirm service availability for your location.

Can you help with both design and ongoing care?

Yes. Our service offering spans landscape planning and installation, outdoor living, irrigation, tree and shrub care, and recurring landscape maintenance. We tailor the scope to your property.

What should I have ready for a consultation?

Your address or ZIP code, a few ideas or photos, the services you are interested in and a general budget range are helpful. You do not need a finished vision to start the conversation.

How much will my project cost?

Every property and project is different. Size, site conditions, materials and scope all influence the investment. We discuss your priorities and budget before defining a tailored proposal.
