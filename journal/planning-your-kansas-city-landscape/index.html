PLANNING · 2 MIN READ

A thoughtful start to your landscape project

The questions worth asking before you choose a single plant or paver.

BY AXEL LANDSCAPING · KANSAS CITY

Begin with the way you live

Before collecting materials or plants, think about your everyday routines. Where do you drink coffee, gather with friends or let the dog out? Which views from inside your home could feel better? A landscape becomes more useful when these small moments shape the plan.

Give the property a closer look

Notice the sun, shade, slopes, existing trees and where water collects after rain. These observations are useful starting points for a site conversation. A plan should respond to real conditions before it settles on an aesthetic.

Set priorities, then a direction

Separate your must-haves from the ideas that could come later. A cohesive plan can help you sequence the work while keeping future patios, planting and lighting connected. Share a comfortable investment range early so the conversation stays practical.

Bring a few references

A handful of photos can reveal a preference for soft planting, crisp structure or warm natural materials. You do not need a finished vision. Knowing what feels like you is enough to begin.
