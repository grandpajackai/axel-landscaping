OUTDOOR LIVING · 2 MIN READ

What makes an outdoor room feel like home?

It starts with proportion, connection and a few beautifully practical details.

BY AXEL LANDSCAPING · KANSAS CITY

Consider the connection

An outdoor room is easier to use when it connects naturally to the home. Think about doors, kitchen access, circulation and the route between seating areas. A comfortable path can matter as much as the patio itself.

Leave room to move

Plan for people as well as furniture. Chairs need space to pull back, guests need a clear route and cooking areas need a comfortable working zone. During design, discuss the furniture you have or hope to use.

Soften the edges

Planting helps a patio settle into the garden. Layers of grasses, shrubs or a small tree can create privacy and a sense of enclosure while keeping the space connected to the wider property.

Think beyond daylight

Subtle lighting can make paths easier to navigate and extend the time you spend outdoors. Focus on useful light and a calm atmosphere, considering how fixtures look from inside the home as well.
